## Normalized observed feature union and proposed response

The sources above demonstrate chapter organization and style transforms; visual outlines and worldbuilding; browser collaboration; EPUB/print PDF export; AI suggestions and generation; self-service publication; and page-flip media. Reedsy provides a direct counterexample to any claim that authoring plus export plus collaboration is novel. Our provisional parity is safe drafting, understandable exports and reliable recovery, not instant parity with all features. The possible differentiation is **portable writing joined to transparent human-curated editorial, rights and finance workflows**; no evidence yet proves users value or competitors lack that combination.

| Capability | Proposed approach | Cost/interoperability concern |
|---|---|---|
| Structured editing and snapshots | Build domain layer; evaluate licensed/open editor | Schema migrations, export fidelity and license review |
| EPUB and print conversion | Integrate/evaluate dedicated tooling in isolated workers | Deterministic output and validator/printer compatibility |
| Payments and distribution | Partner after jurisdiction and contract diligence | Settlement, data portability, retries and reconciliation |
| AI, advanced collaboration, AR | Defer | Consent, provider data terms, complexity and accessibility |

## Hypotheses, not demonstrated unmet needs

No proven unmet need or validated opportunity is asserted. Test: (1) authors fear lost saves; (2) import loses structure; (3) export requires manual repairs; (4) planning context is fragmented; (5) ownership terms are unclear; (6) submission status is opaque; (7) rights scope is hard to understand; (8) proof changes are difficult to reconcile; (9) sales and royalties are difficult to audit; (10) readers cannot reliably access purchased content. DISC-03 provides a test protocol. Validate severity and willingness to switch before making differentiation or investment claims.
